# Assets y prompts: puentes, cascadas y agua

Herramienta: ImageGen integrada, sin CLI ni claves de API. Referencia principal proporcionada por el usuario: **Guía Pixel Art de Puentes y Cascadas (1).png**. Se usó en las seis llamadas como referencia explícita; no se distribuye la guía ni se convierte el mapa en un fondo.

## Inventario final

En `public/assets/`, cada PNG tiene un JSON homónimo con sus frames:

- `bridge-rustic-v1.png`: madera, poste circular con cuerda, viga, apoyo pétreo, flores y nudo.
- `bridge-farm-v1.png`: madera, poste, viga, apoyo, jardinera y vegetación de acceso.
- `bridge-large-v1.png`: madera reforzada, poste con herrajes, viga, pilar pétreo, farol y vegetación.
- `waterfall-main-v1.png`: ocho frames originales de caída, borde rocoso, espuma y gotas.
- `waterfall-small-v1.png`: seis frames originales para los tres desniveles menores.
- `water-depth-v1.png`: agua profunda/media, lecho de grava/rocas y material de orilla.

Los originales permanecen en el directorio de imágenes generadas de Codex. `scripts/prepare-river-assets.py` recorta celdas, conserva alfa y proporciones, registra los frames con una caja compartida y usa nearest-neighbor. El empaquetado PNG usa paleta de 256 colores sin dithering y descarta alfa inferior a 8/255 para evitar ruido invisible; no aplica blur. Los seis PNG suman aproximadamente 774 KiB.

## Prompts finales

### Puente rústico

Use case: stylized-concept / game production sprites. Image 1 is the EXACT primary art reference for Granja Pollito. Reproduce the SMALL RUSTIC BRIDGE construction in its upper-left panel with maximum fidelity: amber/honey wood, carved planks, round timber posts with circular end-grain caps, cream rope railing, moss and tiny flowers. Create a production-ready modular sprite atlas on TRUE TRANSPARENT alpha background, 3 columns x 2 rows, six equal cells with wide transparent padding and no overlap. No text, labels, UI, scenery, river, complete bridge, perspective redesign or borders. Cell top-left: a square seamless honey wooden plank texture swatch seen straight from above (opaque square occupying only middle of cell, boards and warm grain faithfully matching reference). Top-middle: ONE upright cylindrical wooden bridge post in the same 3/4 overhead RPG view, round top end-grain, rope tie and small moss at foot, isolated, entire post visible. Top-right: ONE narrow straight horizontal wooden beam with rope loop and detailed warm grain, isolated, seen in the same overhead RPG view, not a complete railing with posts. Bottom-left: ONE irregular grey stone footing/pier block with moss, isolated in same view, no water. Bottom-middle: ONE small irregular tuft of green grass with tiny white/yellow wildflowers for bridge landings, isolated. Bottom-right: ONE isolated little rope knot/tie on a small wooden mounting peg, same style. All six have the detailed sharp pixel-art rendering and rich handcrafted palette of the attached board; small precisely drawn details, not generic low-res placeholders, no blur or painterly backgrounds. Distinct separated parts for assembling arbitrary-length bridges without stretching or rotating whole bridge sprites.

### Cascada principal

Production pixel-art SPRITESHEET, one original animated MAIN WATERFALL for Granja Pollito. The attached guide's upper right section CASCADA PRINCIPAL is the exact visual design target: rich cyan/blue falling water with white broken currents, mossy grey stone shoulders, irregular foamy lip, turbulent white/aqua splash and spray at bottom. Reproduce that asset faithfully, never simplify it into straight vertical stripes. Output EXACTLY EIGHT animation frames arranged in a perfectly regular 4-column x 2-row grid. Every cell same size, every waterfall same size and same fixed location inside its cell. Each is a tall narrow waterfall: flowing water about 1 wide to 2.5 tall, with a small rocky mossy edge on each side and a wide irregular splash at base. True transparent background, including outside each waterfall and between cells. No dark rectangle, no backdrop scenery, no labels, numbers, text or grid lines. Top edge fixed, stone shoulders fixed, base fixed across all eight frames. Only internal water ribbons, froth, foam and tiny drops vary subtly and cyclically frame to frame; no whole-waterfall translation, no rocking stones, no camera variation. Blue darker crevices, bright cyan translucent-looking strands, irregular white highlights and fine white spray exactly like guide. Bottom foam feathered and broken into droplets, NOT a straight white horizontal cutoff. Detailed crisp pixel art, hard pixel edges, no blur, no realistic photo. Plenty of transparent padding per cell so foam isn't clipped. Animation should loop smoothly over the eight chronological cells, left to right then bottom row. Suitable for a natural waterfall 76 world units wide and about 206 world units tall at normal game scale, with rock shoulders beyond water core.

### Cascada pequeña

Create a production-ready animated SMALL WATERFALL spritesheet for Granja Pollito, matching the attached reference section CASCADA PEQUEÑA and map examples with maximum visual fidelity. Exactly SIX frames arranged in 3 columns x 2 equal rows, generous transparent space between them. Each frame depicts the SAME short natural waterfall, identical position and scale, fixed mossy grey rock shoulders, irregular upper water lip, rich blue/cyan falling water and lively white/aqua broken foam at the base. Compact low-drop form, clearly smaller/shorter than the guide's tall main waterfall. Water falling over one natural mossy rocky step, core water width roughly half of total waterfall height; avoid a wide scenic environment. Sharp detailed pixel art matching the guide, no blur. Across the six chronological frames vary only interior flowing water ribbons, tiny highlights, splash and droplets subtly. Stone shoulders and upper rim stay perfectly still; no whole-sprite translation, no perspective changes. Transparent alpha everywhere outside rock/water/foam silhouettes and in spaces between cells, no dark rectangles, no labels, numbers, text or borders. At the base foam has individual irregular splashes and droplets, blends to clear water, not a horizontal cutoff. Top water rush and foamy transition are visible. No unrelated vegetation, characters or bridge. A loopable six-frame animation of the requested small waterfall, not a generic vertically striped sheet.

Each tile seamless/repeating on its own with sharp detailed pixel clusters, not blur. Top-left tile: deep blue/teal water matching Agua profunda, subtle irregular short cyan ripples and faint light reflections, no visible floor, quiet not stormy. Bottom-left: SHALLOW RIVERBED texture with detailed rounded pebbles/gravel, sand, grey/tan/mossy green stones and tiny sparse submerged plant shadows matching Agua poco profunda con fondo visible. Bottom-right: submerged sand-and-pebble shallow-bank material matching the guide's wet edge, irregular ochre sand, small flat gravel and green moss, no grass clumps above water, no complete shoreline scene. All four compatible with the exact warm lush handcrafted pixel art in the reference, varied clustered organic shapes and fine texture, avoid grid-like repeating dots. Preserve quiet cozy RPG water, neither realistic photography nor generic flat blue squares. Opaque tile atlas for depth-blended chunks, detailed at both close and far game zoom.
